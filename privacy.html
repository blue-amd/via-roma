Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

VIA ROMA respects your privacy. This policy explains how information provided through our website may be collected and used when you contact us or place an order.

1. Information We Collect

We may receive information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, and order details when you submit an inquiry or send an order request.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information you provide may be used to respond to inquiries, process order requests, confirm product details, and communicate with you about your order.

3. How Our Contact and Order Forms Work

Our contact form and Order Now buttons open your own email app with a message already written. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app. Your message is then delivered to us by your email provider, and our website does not store it.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell your personal information. Information may be shared when necessary to respond to your request or comply with applicable legal requirements.

5. Cookies and Third-Party Services

Our website does not ask you to create an account. It loads fonts and design files from outside services, and it is hosted on GitHub Pages. These services may receive basic technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads the page.

6. Data Protection

We take reasonable steps to protect the information provided to us. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. How Long We Keep Information

We keep emails about inquiries and orders only as long as needed to answer you, complete your order, and keep basic order records.

8. Your Choices

You may contact us to ask about the personal information you have provided or request assistance regarding its correction or deletion, subject to applicable requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page along with an updated revision date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact VIA ROMA through our Contact page.
